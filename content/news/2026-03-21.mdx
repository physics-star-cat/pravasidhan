## Headlines

- **Indian Navy deploys warships to Arabian Sea as Iran tensions persist.** INS Vikramaditya carrier group now patrolling shipping lanes near the Strait of Hormuz. Defence Minister called it "precautionary" — which is what they always call it until it isn't.
- **2.68 lakh Indians now repatriated from West Asia.** Up from 2.44 lakh three days ago. Kuwait and Bahrain are seeing the largest outflows. MEA says repatriation flights will continue "as long as necessary."
- **RBI holds rates steady at 6.25%.** No surprise. Governor Das cited "global uncertainty" — the polite way of saying oil at $100 makes everything harder. Next review in April.
- **Lok Sabha reconvenes Monday.** Parliament resumes March 23 after passing Demands for Grants last week. The Opposition wants a debate on repatriation costs. The government wants to talk about infrastructure spending. As is tradition.
- **CBDT extends ITR filing deadline for NRIs to August 31.** Previously July 31. The official reason is "disruptions to international banking channels." The practical reason is that half the Gulf-based filers just lost their jobs.

## Markets
