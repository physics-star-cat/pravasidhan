## Headlines

- **Dated Brent crude hits all-time record at $144 a barrel.** The spot price surged as Trump's deadline for Iran to reopen the Strait of Hormuz approaches with no deal in sight. Futures are trading around $109-113, but the premium for immediately available barrels tells the real story. Morgan Stanley calls the current premiums "exceptional." If you're wondering why petrol prices back home are climbing — this is why.
- **RBI MPC meets today — rate decision tomorrow morning.** Governor Sanjay Malhotra is chairing the first bi-monthly policy review of FY27. Markets widely expect the repo rate held at 5.25% with a neutral stance. The committee is weighing oil shock inflation against slowing growth. The press conference is at noon IST on April 8.
- **New Income Tax Act 2025 is now in effect.** As of April 1, the Income Tax Act 1961 has been replaced entirely. The new act uses simplified language and restructured sections. Form 16 is now Form 130. Rule 9 introduces new methods for estimating NRI India-linked income where exact amounts can't be determined. If you file Indian taxes, the form numbers you memorised are all different now.
- **NRI deposits fall 26% year-on-year.** Total inflows dropped to around $10.6 billion, driven mainly by reduced interest in FCNR accounts as global interest rates stabilise and exchange rate volatility eases. NRE FD rates remain at 6.5-7.25% for 1-3 year tenures — still competitive, but not enough to stem the outflow.
- **Reports of US-Iran ceasefire talks lift sentiment.** Multiple mediators are reportedly discussing terms for a potential 45-day ceasefire. Nothing confirmed, but enough to pull markets off their lows today. The Strait situation remains the single biggest variable for Indian inflation and the rupee.
